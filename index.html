Personal-use application

Private Ledger

Private Ledger is a personal investment record and evidence-review system for its authorised account owner. It helps organise source documents from the owner's Google account and presents reviewable records in a private, authenticated ledger.

How the Google integration works

  • Gmail (read-only): locate relevant correspondence and PDF attachments supplied to the owner's account. The app does not send or delete email.
  • Google Drive (limited file access): read and organise files explicitly available to the app, including storing authorised evidence copies in the owner's Drive archive. Access is not intended to cover the entire Drive.
  • Evidence review: register source identifiers, check for duplicates, and prepare items for owner review. The cloud intake worker cannot independently approve or post financial transactions.

This is an information page, not the private ledger application. It does not provide a login or access to personal financial records.

Privacy and contact

Read the Privacy Policy for details about access, storage, retention, revocation and the limited use of Google data. The app is not offered as a public service; privacy requests can be directed to the application owner through the support contact provided on its Google OAuth consent screen.
